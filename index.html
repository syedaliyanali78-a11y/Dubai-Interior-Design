Who we are

We design for the feeling, not just the photograph.

NOIR ATELIER is a sample luxury interior design studio concept created to demonstrate a refined digital presence for a Dubai-based design business.

We bring together spatial planning, tactile materials, lighting and furniture to create interiors with clarity and character. Every project is approached as a partnership, shaped by the client's brief, lifestyle and practical needs.

  • Concept development & moodboards
  • Material, colour and finish direction
  • Space planning and furniture layouts
  • Styling and finishing guidance
